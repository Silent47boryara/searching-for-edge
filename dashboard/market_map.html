How to read this map and its limitations

Price and OI up means price and open-contract counts rise together. Price and OI down means contracts shrink as price falls; dominant long liquidations add evidence of forced closures. This does not establish a single cause of the move or promise a rebound.

Regimes use the signs of 4-hour price and OI changes. Even a small nonzero move belongs to one of four combinations; this is not a separate trend/range classifier. Funding describes holding conditions; the current rate may change before settlement.

Data: 29.08–14.10.2026. Hourly grid; market charts do not update automatically. Liquidation/tape outages are masked; gamma appears only after confirmed snapshot completion. Available observations may differ across feeds. Future returns and outcomes are not embedded in the map.

Tests of individual feeds and combinations have not established stable improvement in next-four-hour forecasts beyond price and volatility. This map is for studying observations.
